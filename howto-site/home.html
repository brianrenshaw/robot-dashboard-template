How I keep track of my work and my health

My tasks and my health records live in a database on my own Cloudflare account. I add to it and ask it questions from a chat app, usually Claude, and I check on things at a private website. Claude Code wrote all the code. I described what I wanted and told it what was wrong.

Why not just use a chat app?

A chat app is a quick way to add things. I can say “Sam owes me the numbers by Thursday” faster than I can fill in a form. It's a poor place to keep them, though. Chats scroll away, each app remembers things its own way, and when I tried a different app I started over.

So the record lives in a database I own. The chat app is how I get to it. Claude, ChatGPT and GrokBot all connect to the same server and see the same data, so I can switch apps without losing anything.

Why it's free

Cloudflare already runs a huge network for its paying customers. A Worker doesn't get a server that sits idle waiting for you. It starts when a request comes in and is done a few milliseconds later, so a hobby project costs them close to nothing. It's also a bet: people who try it on side projects bring it to work later.

You don't need a domain

Every account gets a free address ending in workers.dev, and it works fine. If you want your own name, I'd buy it at Hover and point it at Cloudflare. Either way it stays private: Cloudflare Access puts a sign-in in front, and only your email gets the code.
